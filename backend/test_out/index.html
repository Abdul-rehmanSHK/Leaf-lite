WELCOME TO MARINE CONSTRUCTION LLC

Waterfront Construction Built Around Your Property & Lifestyle.

Your waterfront is more than a property feature. It is where boat days begin, family and friends gather, and lasting lake memories are made. It also requires the right construction, protection and ongoing care. Marine Construction LLC helps homeowners, property owners and local marinas enhance, protect and enjoy their waterfronts with professionally designed docks, boat and Jet Ski lifts, seawalls, boardwalks and shoreline-stabilization solutions. Since 1982, our locally owned company has served the region with experienced crews, purpose-built marine equipment and practical knowledge of local shorelines, water levels and permitting requirements. We manage projects of varying sizes and complexity—from targeted repairs to complete waterfront transformations.
